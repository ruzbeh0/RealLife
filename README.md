# Real Life

Real Life lets you control how fast citizens progress through life and education stages in-game.

## What it changes

### Age settings

- Child school start age.
- Child → Teen age limit.
- Teen → Adult age limit.
- Retirement age.
- Female/Male life expectancy.

### Education settings

- College degree share at university capacity.
- Years spent in College and University.
- Graduation probabilities for:
  - Elementary
  - High school
  - College
  - University
- High-school entry and adult high-school entry rates.
- Worker continuing education probability.

### Birth and relationships

- Student birth rate
- Base birth rate
- Adult female birth rate bonus
- Divorce rate
- Partner-search rate

## Important note

- This mod changes population lifecycle and has a **risk of a death wave** when applied to existing cities.
